## Welcome

Welcome to the OWASP Addis Ababa chapter homepage. The chapter leaders are [Kiya Tolcha Batula](mailto:kiya.batula@owasp.org), [Yohannes Beneberu Admasu](mailto:yohannes.admasu@owasp.org), and [Abnet Mekuria Tadesse](mailto:abnet.tadesse@owasp.org).

OWASP Addis Ababa is a local Ethiopian chapter for developers, security professionals, students, and anyone interested in application security. We are restarting chapter activity to build a stronger local community around secure software.

Our mission is to raise application security awareness in Addis Ababa and help people learn, share, and contribute through free, open community events.

## Meetings

Everyone is welcome to join our chapter meetings. Meetings are **free and open to the public**. You do not need to be an OWASP member to attend.

We plan to run a mix of in-person meetups in Addis Ababa and virtual sessions. Typical meetings include talks, workshops, and networking.

Please [consider joining OWASP](/membership/) if you find our community, projects, and meetings valuable.

## Speaking at OWASP Addis Ababa

We welcome local people to give talks and share how they are securing software. If you would like to present, please review the [OWASP Speaker Agreement](/www-policy/legal/speaker-agreement) and contact the chapter leaders with:

- Proposed talk title
- Short abstract
- Preferred format (talk or hands-on workshop)

Vendor pitches are not allowed. Please keep company logos off all but one introductory slide.

## Meeting Supporters

We are looking for organizations in Addis Ababa that can support the chapter by providing meeting space, refreshments, or other in-kind support.

If you are interested in supporting the chapter, please get in touch with one of the chapter leaders.

## Local Community

We welcome collaboration with universities, technology communities, and local security groups in Addis Ababa. If you organize a related meetup or student group, please contact the leaders so we can work together.

## Participation

The Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) is a nonprofit foundation that works to improve the security of software. All of our projects, tools, documents, forums, and chapters are free and open to anyone interested in improving application security.

Chapters are led by local leaders in accordance with the [Chapters Policy](/www-policy/operational/chapters). Financial contributions should only be made online using the authorized online donation button. To be a speaker at any OWASP chapter, review the [speaker agreement](/www-policy/legal/speaker-agreement) and then contact the local chapter leader with details of what OWASP project, independent research, or related software security topic you would like to present.

Everyone is welcome and encouraged to participate in our [Projects](/projects/), [Local Chapters](/chapters/), [Events](/events/), [Online Groups](https://groups.google.com/a/owasp.com/){:target="_blank"}, and [Community Slack Channel](https://owasp.slack.com/){:target="_blank"}. We especially encourage diversity in all our initiatives. OWASP is a fantastic place to learn about application security, to network, and even to build your reputation as an expert. We also encourage you to [become a member](/membership/) or consider a [donation](/donate/) to support our ongoing work.
