## Get Involved

OWASP Addis Ababa is community driven. There are several ways to contribute:

### Attend Events

Join our free meetups, workshops, and discussions. Membership is not required to attend.

### Speak at a Meeting

If you would like to present a talk or run a workshop, review the [OWASP Speaker Agreement](/www-policy/legal/speaker-agreement) and contact the chapter leaders with your talk title and a short abstract.

### Volunteer

Help with event organization, venue coordination, outreach, or supporting first-time attendees.

### Support the Chapter

Organizations can support the chapter with meeting space or other in-kind contributions. Please contact the chapter leaders.

### Join the Global Community

* [OWASP Projects](/projects/)
* [OWASP Slack](https://owasp.slack.com/)
* [Become a Member](/membership/)
